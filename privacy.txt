Privacy Policy

Alippe is a children's alphabet app made by Madi Yedenbay. It teaches the Kazakh letters and plays a fairy tale. The app works on the device and does not ask the child to create an account.

Information collection

Alippe does not collect personal information, does not show ads, and does not send usage logs, device identifiers, or location to the developer. Pictures and sounds are stored inside the app.

Children

The app is made for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or other third-party services that collect data. The system stores that distribute the app (Google Play, the App Store) have their own privacy policies.

Changes

This policy is effective as of 2026-11-24. Updates will be posted in this file.
